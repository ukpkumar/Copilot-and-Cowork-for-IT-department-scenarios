# Lab 3: Scheduled IT operations briefing

[Previous: Lab 2](lab-02-create-and-share-a-skill.md) | [Labs](README.md) | [Home](../README.md) | Next: [Lab 4](lab-04-build-a-cowork-app.md)

**Use case:** [UC-01 Daily IT operations briefing](../use-cases/cowork/UC-01-daily-it-operations-briefing.md)
**Reference file:** [Contoso-IT-Practice-Data-Workbook.xlsx](../reference-files/Contoso-IT-Practice-Data-Workbook.xlsx), `Incidents` and `Service_KPIs` tabs (save it in your OneDrive, for example in `IT Reports`)

## Task 1: Run the briefing once

```text
Prepare my IT operations briefing for this morning's 08:30 stand-up from the workbook
in my OneDrive folder IT Reports (Incidents and Service_KPIs tabs).
1. Headline: overall status in one sentence (green, amber or red)
2. Open P1 and P2 incidents: ID, service, site, age, assignment group
3. SLA breaches this month
4. Service desk backlog trend over the last 8 weeks
5. Three decisions or escalations for today
One page. Use a table for incidents. Do not send anything.
```

## Task 2: Refine

```text
Put the decisions first and show remote sites in bold.
```

## Task 3: Schedule it

```text
Run this briefing every working day, Monday to Friday, at 07:15 Dubai time.
Show it to me only. Do not send email or post in Teams.
```

Check the scheduled task in Cowork: name, schedule and prompt.

## Task 4: Tidy up

Pause or delete the scheduled task at the end of the lab if you do not need it.

## What good looks like

- [ ] Two P2 incidents that breached SLA (INC-24095 and INC-24108) are listed.
- [ ] The backlog rise over the last two weeks is called out.
- [ ] The scheduled task runs on weekdays at 07:15 Dubai time and sends nothing.
- [ ] You know where to pause or delete it.
