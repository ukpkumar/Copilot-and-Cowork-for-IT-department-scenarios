# Contoso KB article

## Steps
1. Identify the problem from the user's point of view (symptom, not cause).
2. Decide the audience: Employees (plain language) or Service Desk (technical steps allowed).
3. Write the article in the format below.
4. Suggest a title that matches how users describe the problem.
5. List the tickets or sources used.

## Format
- **KB ID**: [Assigned on publish]
- **Title**: starts with a verb, for example "Connect to VPN from a remote site"
- **Applies to**: devices, sites, user groups
- **Steps**: numbered, one action per step, no more than 10
- **If this does not work**: when to raise a ticket in the IT Self-Service Portal, and the category to choose. For urgent issues, call the 24x7 service desk on extension 7000.
- **Last updated**: today's date

## Rules
- Never include passwords, keys, internal IP addresses or admin-only steps in Employee articles.
- One article per problem.
- Mark any step you could not confirm from the sources with [Confirm].
